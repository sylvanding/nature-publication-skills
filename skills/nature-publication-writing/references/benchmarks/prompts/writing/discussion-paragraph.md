# Discussion Paragraph Benchmark Prompt

## Task

Draft one Discussion paragraph that states contribution, strongest evidence, limitations, and next use.

## Source packet

```text
Contribution: confidence-aware super-resolution for live-cell imaging
Strongest evidence: input-output montage, error maps, calibration curve, long-term movie validation
Limitations: tested on selected fluorescence movies; no animal data; no clinical endpoint
Future use: choosing frames or regions that need manual review
```

## Expected checks

- Synthesizes rather than repeats Results.
- Names the strongest evidence without adding new claims.
- Gives concrete limitations and future use.
- Avoids overclaiming generality.

## Do not invent

Do not invent translational impact, universal generalization, or new experiments.

## Missing inputs

Ask for external validation and failure cases if the user wants a stronger limitations paragraph.
