# Introduction Paragraph Benchmark Prompt

## Task

Draft one introduction paragraph that moves from broad need to a concrete technical barrier.

## Source packet

```text
Topic: high-resolution 3D fluorescence microscopy
Need: observing subcellular dynamics in thick samples
Barrier: sparse views, phototoxicity, noise, slow reconstruction
Available evidence: physics-guided reconstruction and line-profile validation
Boundary: no claim about all imaging modalities
```

## Expected checks

- Starts with an accessible field-level need.
- Replaces vague challenge language with specific barriers.
- Ends with a gap that motivates the method.
- Does not preview results unsupported by the packet.

## Do not invent

Do not invent literature consensus, citations, instrument specifications, or biological discoveries.

## Missing inputs

Ask for exact prior methods and measured constraints if the paragraph needs citations.
